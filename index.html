Tool Examples

Internal tools for a fictional packaging company. All data is made up.

Start here

FreestyleSwimlane Creator grew into Freestyle, a free swimlane diagram tool with its own repo. Annotation GeneratorLoads an artwork revision by UPC and downloads an Illustrator script that draws its print annotations. Mockup Jsx GeneratorGenerates an Illustrator script that draws a mockup dieline from a request's dimensions. Sales LookupAnswers whether a bag has been made before, searching by layers, format, style, gusset, size and weight. Sample ReviewPhone-friendly checklist for reviewing production samples. Mockup Request FormStep-by-step form that creates a mockup request with validation rules. Field Id ExplorerLists a table's fields, IDs and latest values for handoff to developers or AI tools.

Everything else

Apps HubPortfolio page of internal apps, with favorites and time-study stats. Art Revision LookupFinds every artwork revision for a product by UPC, newest first. Artwork GalleryVisual search of past packaging artwork, filtered by customer, brand, format, style, gusset and weight. Artwork ReviewerCompares two artwork versions side by side or overlaid, with synchronized zoom and panning. Checklist TestingFind an art checklist, see its tasks by department and status, and start and stop timers. Density CalculatorCalculates a product's density from weighed samples and saves it to the database. Fill Density ConverterCalculates how much test fill simulates a product's weight and density in a bag. Fill RecordingsRecords known good fills per bag and recommends bag size, product weight or density from the other two. Font JammerBulk-uploads font files, checking each has the characters the annotations print. Illustrator Account PanelShared sign-in panel for a suite of Illustrator extensions.
