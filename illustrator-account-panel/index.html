Where to get your token
  1. Open your user tokens page in Quickbase (sign in if it asks). You can also get there from your name (top right) → My preferences → Manage my user tokens.
  2. Click New user token, name it "Illustrator panels", assign it to the Demo Packaging app, and save.
  3. Copy the token and paste it above.
The token is you: the panels can only see and do what your own Quickbase account is allowed to. It gives no extra access.
