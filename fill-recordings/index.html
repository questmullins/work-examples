      How this is worked out
      • Each recording is a real bag, filled with FillBead to a level that looked right.
      • “Usual” is the middle value of the recorded bags in a group, so one odd bag does not swing it.
      • Room for product comes from the weight of FillBead that went in (FillBead weighs 23.52 lb per cubic foot).
      • A filled bag rounds out, so how much it holds follows the length of its walls (width + gusset), not width × gusset. The planner learns how round each style fills from recorded bags, leaning on the ones closest in size, and names the closest one so you can check it.
      • The usual fill comes from recorded bags of about the same height.
      • A fill is kept below the zipper when the zipper's position is known.
      • Similar bags are tested bags whose width, height and gusset are each within 2 in of the planned bag. Their artwork comes from the newest order for that barcode.
      • Which machine makes the bag: a non-terminated quad with no zipper or other feature goes on the Bag Machine Quad; any other quad goes on the Totani BH60 if it fits, otherwise on the BH80; stand-up pouches go on the Totani CT60; for rollstock only the width is checked, against the 1,200 mm web. Microperfs don't count as a feature.
