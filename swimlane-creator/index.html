How this works

Draw the process on the Board the way you would in Arcweave. The Swimlane beside it is the same map, arranged for you.

On the board

  • Add a card: double-click empty space, or use + Step and + Decision (also under Edit).
  • Write in it: double-click the card. Enter finishes, Shift+Enter adds a line, Esc cancels.
  • Connect: drag from a blue dot on a card to another card. Drop on empty space to make the next card. The arrow leaves from the dot you dragged.
  • Shape an arrow: click it. Drag an end dot round its card to change the side it leaves or arrives on (or onto another card). Drag the middle dot to bend it; double-click that dot to straighten. While you drag, the side it will attach to lights up in blue.
  • Owners: set them all up with Edit, Owners. Double-click a card's coloured header to pick its owner.
  • Links: give a step a Link and a link icon appears on its box; click it to open the page.
  • Keys: select a step and give it a time ("2 days", "4-5 weeks"): the Map panel adds up how long the whole process takes, and each step shows how long from it to the end. Something broken records a problem at that step (a red mark). A note is a small cloud on the box that opens when you point at it. Printed swimlanes list the problems and notes underneath.
  • Label an arrow: double-click it, or select it and type in the panel.
  • One at a time: click a card or an arrow and it is the only thing that responds; the rest fade a little. Click empty space to let go of it.
  • Select several: drag a box around them, or Shift-click. Drag one to move them all.
  • Both views: the toolbar shows the board, the swimlane or both, side by side or top and bottom, and hides or shows the side panel.
  • Sharing: View has the viewer link, opens the swimlane as a viewer sees it, and sets who can view and edit. The same choices are in Save to Quickbase. Someone who may only look gets the viewer, even from an editor link.
  • Lanes: drag a lane by the dotted grip beside its name to move it up or down.
  • Move around: hold Space and drag (or drag with the right mouse button). Scroll to zoom.
  • Delete removes, Ctrl+D duplicates, Ctrl+Z undoes, arrow keys nudge.

How a card becomes a swimlane box

  • The owner is the lane. Pick who does the step in the panel on the right.
  • A question is a decision. End the text with "?" or choose Decision; it becomes a diamond.
  • Start labels with Yes or No. Yes arrows are green and No arrows red. An arrow that goes back to an earlier step is a loop, in yellow.
  • The ideal path is the shortest way through over plain and Yes arrows. It gets the blue glow. You can force it on or off per arrow.
  • Phases: select the cards of a phase (drag a box round them), then choose "Phase for all" in the panel or right-click one of them. Or right-click the first card of a phase and choose "Start a new phase here". Name phases in the Map panel (click empty space to see it).
  • Order in time follows the arrows. Where two cards could go either way, the one further left on the board comes first.

From Arcweave

  • Export the project as JSON. If it arrives as a zip, unzip it and open project_settings.json with Open (or drop it on the board).
  • Card title = owner. Card text = the step. A card with "?" or a branch = a decision. "[P2]" at the start of a card starts phase 2.
  • Open a newer export of the same board later and this map is updated in place: your phases, pins, arrow sides, colours and box sizes are kept.

Getting it out

  • File, Print: one letter-landscape page per phase, or the whole process on one wide page. Choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.
  • Images: a PNG of each phase and of the whole process, to copy or download.
  • Save to Quickbase checks the map is complete, then saves it and a PDF of its phase pages to the Swimlanes table. Load swimlane lists every saved swimlane you can open, and filters them by department or name.
  • Under File, Export JSON downloads the map as a file, and Import JSON loads one back. Copy or paste JSON shows the same thing as text, to keep in a Quickbase field or send to someone. The map is also kept in this browser between visits.
