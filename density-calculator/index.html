How to take the measurements
  1. Gather suppliesStraight edge, calibrated ¼ ft³ density cup, a scale set to pounds, and sample product.
  2. Tare the scaleSet the empty cup on the scale and press Tare/Zero.
  3. Fill the cupLoad product until it overflows, tap the base firmly to settle it, then level off the top with the straight edge.
  4. Weigh itRead the weight in pounds and enter it here.
  5. RepeatEmpty the cup and repeat the fill and weigh steps until you have 5 weights.
  6. Review and saveThe calculator averages the weights, multiplies by 4, and moves the decimal one place right. Example: 0.25, 0.24, 0.25, 0.26, 0.24 → avg 0.248 → 0.992 → 9.92 lbs/ft³.
